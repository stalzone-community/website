---
title: Search from an item's tab now stays on that tab
type: improvement
area: tools
---
Comparing two rifles used to mean four clicks. Search from a tab — Attachments,
Craft, Auction, any of them — and the item you pick opens on the same tab
instead of on its overview, so you can walk through a dozen items looking at the
one thing you came for.

If the item you pick has no such tab, it opens on its overview as before.
