---
title: How busy is the Zone right now
type: feature
area: site
---
A live count of players in game, and a seven-day chart behind it, so you can see
whether you are logging in at peak or at four in the morning. Times are shown in
your own timezone.

Numbers are Steam's, by way of [SteamCharts](https://steamcharts.com/); they
count the whole game rather than any one region.
