title: Stalzone opens
type: feature
area: site
impact: major
---
Every item in the game, in one place you can actually search: 2 311 weapons,
armor, artefacts, attachments and consumables across 14 categories, with full
stats, upgrade levels and what fits what.

The whole catalogue is built from EXBO's own item database, so it refreshes on
patch day rather than whenever somebody gets round to editing a wiki page.
