---
title: A page per item, with every level of it
type: feature
area: database
---
Each item gets its own page: the full stat block, every upgrade level side by
side, and the deltas between them, so you can see what a level actually buys you
before you spend on it.

Weapons list the attachments and modules they take, armor lists what it protects
against, and artefacts show what they give you and what they cost.
