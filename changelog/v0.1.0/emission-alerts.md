Subscribe on the [emissions page](/emission) and your browser tells you when one
starts and when it is safe to go outside again — on desktop and on a phone,
per region.

Live emission timings need production API access from EXBO, which is still
pending; the alert plumbing is in place and starts firing the day it lands.
