---
title: The overview page now opens on what is happening
type: feature
area: site
impact: major
---
The front page used to be a grid of categories and a sentence. It now leads with
how busy the game is — concurrent players on Steam over the last seven days,
hover any hour for the exact figure — and follows it with the next emission and
the builds people are publishing.

The categories moved to a column on the right, each with its own mark and
colour, alongside what is new on the site.
