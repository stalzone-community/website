---
title: Pick your region, and see what an artefact actually is
type: feature
area: market
impact: major
---
Auction prices are per region, and until now everyone saw Europe. There is a
region chip in the top bar — RU, EU, NA or SEA — and it sticks. Worth changing
if you play elsewhere: Russia carries several times the listings Europe does, so
the same artefact can go from "nothing on sale" to a full page of offers.

Artefact listings now show what is being sold, not just what it costs. Quality,
upgrade bonus and any rolled properties sit beside the price, so two listings of
the same artefact at different prices are finally telling you why.
